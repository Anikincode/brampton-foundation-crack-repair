Basement Leak Repair in Brampton

Water on the basement floor is the symptom, not the problem. There are only a handful of places water can get into a basement, and the right repair depends on which one it is. We find the entry point first, then fix that.

Basement leak repairPage 12

How do you fix a leaking basement? First find where the water enters: a wall crack, the joint where the wall meets the floor, a window well, a tie-rod hole or a pipe opening. A single crack is sealed with injection from inside. Water along the wall-floor joint usually needs interior drainage to a sump pump. Widespread wall seepage needs exterior waterproofing.

Below: where water gets in and the fix for each, what you can check yourself first, interior vs. exterior waterproofing, and what it costs.

What can you check before calling anyone?

Some basement leaks are fixed with a shovel and a downspout extension. Check these first:

  • Downspouts. They should empty well away from the house. About 2 metres is a common rule of thumb.
  • Grading. The soil next to the foundation should slope away from the house, not toward it.
  • Eavestroughs. Clogged troughs overflow and dump water right against the wall.
  • Window wells. Clear out leaves and debris so water can drain.
  • Sump pump. If you have one, pour a bucket of water into the pit and make sure the pump starts. Signs it's failing are on our sump pump page.

If water still comes in after that, it's getting through the foundation itself, and that's what we fix.

Interior or exterior waterproofing?

Both stop water from reaching your living space. They do it in different places.

  • Interior waterproofing catches water at the footing inside the basement and pumps it out. No digging outside, it works in winter, and it usually costs less.
  • Exterior waterproofing stops water at the outside face of the wall. It means digging down to the footing, so it costs more, but the wall itself stays dry.

What does basement leak repair cost?

It ranges from a few hundred dollars to seal one crack to tens of thousands for a full waterproofing system. Published Canadian guides put interior crack injection at $500 to $1,500 per crack and a full interior system for a 1,000-square-foot basement at roughly $10,000 to $32,000.

New home? Call your builder first

In Ontario, water coming through the basement or foundation walls is covered by the Tarion two-year warranty on new homes. Dampness alone isn't covered, but actual water leaking in is. See Tarion's coverage page.

Does home insurance cover a leaking basement?

Usually not when water seeps in gradually through the foundation. Sewer backup and overland flooding are often separate add-ons to a policy. Check your own policy wording before you assume either way.

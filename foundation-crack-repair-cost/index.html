Foundation Crack Repair Cost in Ontario (2026)

You found a crack. Maybe it's wet. The first question is the obvious one: how much is this going to cost?

Short answer

How much does foundation crack repair cost? In Ontario, sealing one foundation crack from inside with injection typically costs $500 to $1,500. An exterior repair, where the soil is dug out and the wall is sealed from outside, typically costs $3,000 to $5,500 per crack. Crack length, access and wall type move the price most.

Below: the typical price ranges, what pushes a quote up or down, what's usually extra, and when it's fine to do nothing.

What changes the price?

  • Length. Ports go every few inches. A longer crack takes more ports, more resin and more time.
  • Inside or outside. Interior injection needs a ladder and a pump. Exterior repair needs digging down to the footing, which is why it costs several times more.
  • A finished basement. If drywall and framing cover the crack, they have to come off and go back on. Ask whether that's in the quote. Often it isn't.
  • Wall type. Poured concrete can be injected. Concrete block usually can't, so it gets sealed a different way.
  • How many cracks. Two cracks done on one visit usually cost less than two separate visits.
  • What's in the way outside. Decks, interlock, driveways and gardens over the dig area add to an exterior job.

What's usually included, and what costs extra?

A crack injection quote usually covers the ports, the resin, the injection and the cleanup.

Ask before you sign about:

  • Removing and replacing drywall, insulation or framing
  • Moving shelving or stored items away from the wall
  • For exterior work: restoring landscaping, interlock or a deck
  • The warranty: how long, and whether it moves to the next owner if you sell

Repair it, DIY it, or leave it alone?

Leave it and watch it if it's a thin, dry, vertical crack in poured concrete. Mark both ends in pencil with the date and check again in a few months.

Try a DIY kit only on a thin, dry crack in a poured wall. Kits don't work on block walls, and they're hard to get right on a crack that's actively leaking.

Get it repaired if water comes in, you're about to finish the basement, or you're selling. A leak behind new drywall turns a few hundred dollars of crack repair into a wet-wall problem.

Get a structural check first if the crack is horizontal, wider than 1/4 inch, or getting wider. See is my foundation crack serious?

How do you get an accurate quote?

Send us:

  1. A clear photo of the crack with a tape measure or coin beside it
  2. Roughly how long the crack is
  3. Whether it leaks, and when (every rain, or only in spring)
  4. Whether the wall is finished, unfinished, or partly covered
